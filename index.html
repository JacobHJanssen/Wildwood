Golf Cart Rental Terms & Notice

1. Operator Requirements & Responsibility

  • Licensing: The operator of the golf cart must possess a valid driver's license.
  • Familiarization: It is the sole responsibility of the operator to be familiar with the safe operation of the cart. If you are uncomfortable or unsure how to operate the vehicle, you must request assistance from Wildwood Country Club staff before departing.
  • Liability for Damages: The operator accepts full responsibility for the cart during the entire rental period. The operator agrees to pay for any damages, repair costs, or replacement fees that result from misuse or negligence.

2. Operational Rules

  • Safe Usage: Carts must be operated in a safe and courteous manner at all times. Horseplay, racing, and reckless driving are strictly prohibited and will result in the immediate forfeiture of rental privileges without refund.
  • Designated Areas: Carts must remain strictly on designated paths and roadways within the Wildwood Country Club property. Off-road driving is prohibited.
  • Passenger Safety: A maximum of two persons may occupy a cart at one time. Passengers must remain seated while the cart is in motion, and all extremities (arms, legs, feet) must remain inside the vehicle at all times.
  • Maintenance & Cleanliness: Any malfunctions must be reported to management immediately upon return. Please ensure all trash and personal items are removed from the cart after use.

3. Assumption of Risk & Release of Liability

The operation of a golf cart involves inherent risks, including but not limited to: rollover, collisions with stationary or moving objects, ejection from the vehicle, or being struck by flying objects such as golf balls. By operating or riding in this equipment, you voluntarily assume all known and unknown risks associated with its use. You hereby fully and forever waive, release, and relinquish any and all claims, demands, or actions against Wildwood Country Club, its officers, agents, volunteers, and employees arising out of or in connection with your use of the golf cart.

4. Indemnification

You agree to indemnify, hold harmless, and defend Wildwood Country Club from any and all claims, actions, damages, or losses sustained by you, your passengers, or third parties arising out of or associated with your use of the golf cart, except in instances involving the gross negligence or willful misconduct of the Club.
